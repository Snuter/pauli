# Vision

Make Open Access science from Europe PMC queryable by patients in plain language, without hallucination, with care for critical information.[^user-journey]

Primary user: person living with a chronic / rare condition (e.g. ME/CFS, fatigue, post-exertional malaise / PEM) tracking research over time.[^user-journey]

Non-goals: diagnosis, treatment recommendation, symptom triage. Research information only, not medical advice.[^user-journey]
